How to Create & Play

Step 1 of 3
✍️

1. Create Your Questions

Click "Add Custom Question" above to enter your question text, 4 answer options, and select the radio button next to the correct answer.

Step 2 of 3
📦

2. Build Up to 50 Questions

Add up to 50 questions for your quiz session. Questions are stored temporarily in your local session and reload will reset cleanly.

Step 3 of 3
🎯

3. Play & Track Your Score

Click "Add & Start Quiz" to play! Use keys 1–4 or A–D to answer, press Enter/Space to advance, enjoy audio effects, and compete for top ranks on the Leaderboard!
